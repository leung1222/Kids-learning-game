Tips for grown-ups

  • Low accuracy + many hints: the idea is not secure yet. Sit together, ask “What method are you trying?”, and practise that skill slowly without rushing to the next level.
  • Very fast + nearly all correct: the work may be too easy. Celebrate, then invite the next level or a harder subject when they feel ready.
  • One level with long time or many wrong tries: treat that as the learning target for a short follow-up session (10–15 minutes), not a whole-subject problem.
  • How to use this report: look for patterns across a few days. Praise effort and strategy (“You used the hint thoughtfully”) more than scores alone.
